Internship · Applied AI · Private implementation

Multi-tenant AI Copilot

At Precisio Technologies, I worked on a multi-tenant AI copilot in Python, FastAPI, and MCP. The design kept raw database access out of the model’s reach, then tested the outputs against prompt-injection and data-leakage risks.

Reported outcome · 45% faster retrieval; PDF transcription reduced data-entry time by 80%.

What I would show in a case study

The approved-query boundary, tenant isolation, adversarial tests, retrieval evaluation, and what changed after real user feedback. Client code and data are not public.

Founder / JAS · Customer workflow

QR Event Ticketing

I built a QR ticketing workflow for an event with 800+ attendees. The difficult part was not generating codes; it was reconciling scanners, pass types, and patchy connectivity when people were already at the door.

Lesson · A working MVP still needs a safer concurrency and offline story.

Where the next version goes

Move the source of truth to a transactional database; make scans idempotent, queue offline events, and resolve conflicting check-ins explicitly. This is a design reflection, not a claim that those changes are already shipped.

Independent research · Data / ML

Portfolio Exposure Classifier

My quantum-portfolio exposure classifier used rolling regressions, sector proxies, and market controls to test whether the basket behaved more like technology or defence/aerospace.

Reported check · 23 of 24 stock-scenario train/holdout results were stable across tested windows.

What I would publish next

The reproducible notebook, split rules, sensitivity plots, and a clear explanation of what the classifier cannot establish. No trading advice.

02 / Approach

Engineering Approach

Customer-facing engineering begins with the workflow. My approach connects discovery, implementation, reliability testing, and feedback.

  1. 01 / DiscoverWatch the work.

    Find the friction, the owner, and the non-negotiable constraints.

  2. 02 / ShapeMake the smallest honest prototype.

    Define what the system will do—and where it must stop.

  3. 03 / HardenTest the uncomfortable path.

    Tenant boundaries, bad inputs, failure recovery, handoff, and cost.

  4. 04 / LearnMeasure use, not applause.

    Observe the workflow after launch and document what changed.

03 / Small interactive studio

Interactive AI Safety Demo

A tiny, deterministic sketch of a useful AI boundary. It is not the Precisio system or a live AI service: choose a request to see what a deliberately narrow tool may return.

SCOPED RESPONSE / EXAMPLE DATA

A summary can be useful without exposing a record.

Allowed: return an aggregate, sourced from a pre-approved view. Example: “12 tickets were resolved this week.” No row-level customer data is sent to the assistant.

Boundary: the tool only exposes an approved aggregate; it never runs arbitrary SQL.

A front-end teaching sketch with invented sample data. It makes no network requests and processes no personal information.
